## What changed for Claim 4d

For C=1200, N=970, HP-2000, the directed finite-source residual/gap bound is
**3.03494e-533**, replacing the historical point measurement **2.00727e-533**.
The retained calculation fails the original `<2.01e-533` comparison. That
failure remains preserved. The revised comparison requires the audited
arithmetic method, even-sector isolated indices 0/1, and the displayed six
significant digits; it passes on the authenticated retained response.

The natural/even eigenvalue comparison is a separate measurement. The October
23 run recorded a relative difference of `3.60615e-524` and passed the `1e-8`
tolerance then in use. The manuscript's reference pair gives `7.927e-526`.
The v2.7 printed-value check would reject the October result for that cell.
Its rounded console value alone cannot establish which pair is more accurate.

Independent directed residual evaluation and analytic even-sector matrix
assembly support the revised calculation. The positive gaps still agree with
the published table at its displayed precision. The old raw matrix and vector
were unavailable for a complete replay of their original producer. See
[Research evidence](RESEARCH_EVIDENCE.md) for source digests and audit scope.

## What completion does and does not establish

- Numerical reproduction uses the documented tolerances. It does not require
  every output bit or arithmetic-floor residual to equal a historical value.
- Some acquired assessments retain conditional energy bounds, finite-interpolant
  scope, unavailable sufficient bounds, or unresolved rows. A completed artifact
  is not automatically a resolved mathematical conclusion.
- Claim 1c at HP-1000 does not establish the selected state's ground index.
- Claim 7 has 84 certified finite-source roots out of 90 computed roots.
  At N=10, ordinals 3–5 at both precisions remain computed but uncertified
  under their source and pole-range scope.
- Cross-configuration comparison and dimension/precision-budget analyses were
  intentionally deferred. Sector-gap certificates were intentionally disabled
  in these individual Ultra runs. Neither is claimed complete here.
- The independent analytic finite-matrix and root certificates in
  [Final verification](FINAL_VERIFICATION.md) retain their historical producer
  pins and proof scopes. They were not all rerun with the current software.

The public manuscript and evidence distinguish these qualifications from
missing execution or publication work. No remaining individual claim rerun is
required for this manuscript update.
